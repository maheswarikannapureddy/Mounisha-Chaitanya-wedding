OUR SACRED TRADITIONS

The Telugu Wedding Ceremony

Every ritual in a Telugu wedding carries its own blessing, symbolism and meaning.

TIMELESS TRADITIONS

Sacred Rituals & Their Meaning

Gowri Puja

గౌరీ పూజ

The bride seeks the blessings of Goddess Gowri for harmony, happiness and a prosperous married life.

Jeelakarra Bellam

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

Cumin and jaggery represent two lives becoming inseparable, sharing both the sweet and challenging moments of life.

Mangalyadharanam

మాంగల్యధారణ

The tying of the mangalsutra marks the marital bond, commitment and lifelong companionship.

Talambralu

తలంబ్రాలు

The couple showers sacred rice over one another, symbolizing joy, abundance and prosperity.

Saptapadi

సప్తపది

Seven sacred steps represent seven promises for partnership, strength, family and shared life.

Kanyadanam

కన్యాదానం

A meaningful family blessing that represents trust, love and the joining of two families.
