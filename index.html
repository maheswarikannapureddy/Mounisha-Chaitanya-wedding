Sacred Telugu Wedding Rituals

🪔

Gowri Puja

గౌరీ పూజ

The bride seeks blessings for harmony, happiness and a beautiful married life.

🌿

Jeelakarra Bellam

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

Cumin and jaggery symbolize two lives becoming inseparable through all of life's moments.

📿

Mangalyadharanam

మాంగల్యధారణ

The mangalsutra represents commitment, companionship and the marital bond.

🌾

Talambralu

తలంబ్రాలు

The sacred rice shower symbolizes joy, prosperity, abundance and playfulness.

👣

Saptapadi

సప్తపది

Seven sacred steps represent seven promises for a shared and meaningful life together.

🤲

Kanyadanam

కన్యాదానం

A family blessing representing love, trust and the joining of two families.
