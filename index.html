TIMELESS TRADITIONS

Sacred Telugu Wedding Rituals

🪔

Gowri Puja

గౌరీ పూజ

The bride seeks Goddess Gowri's blessings for harmony, happiness and a beautiful married life.

🌿

Jeelakarra Bellam

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

Cumin and jaggery symbolize two lives coming together and remaining united through life's sweet and challenging moments.

📿

Mangalyadharanam

మాంగల్యధారణ

The tying of the mangalsutra signifies love, commitment and the beginning of married life.

🌾

Talambralu

తలంబ్రాలు

The couple showers sacred rice over one another, symbolizing prosperity, joy and abundance.

👣

Saptapadi

సప్తపది

Seven sacred steps represent seven promises for companionship, prosperity, family and lifelong partnership.

🤲

Kanyadanam

కన్యాదానం

A meaningful family blessing that represents the joining of two families and the beginning of a new chapter.
