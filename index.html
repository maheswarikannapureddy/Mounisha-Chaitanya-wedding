TIMELESS BLESSINGS

Sacred Rituals & Their Meaning

🪔

Gowri Puja

గౌరీ పూజ

The bride seeks the blessings of Goddess Gowri for a happy, harmonious and prosperous married life.

🌿

Jeelakarra Bellam

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

Cumin and jaggery symbolize a bond that remains inseparable through life's sweet and challenging moments.

📿

Mangalyadharanam

మాంగల్యధారణ

The groom ties the mangalsutra, symbolizing commitment, companionship and the marital bond.

🌾

Talambralu

తలంబ్రాలు

The couple showers sacred rice over each other, representing joy, abundance and prosperity.

👣

Saptapadi

సప్తపది

The seven sacred steps represent promises of strength, prosperity, family, friendship and lifelong partnership.

🤲

Kanyadanam

కన్యాదానం

The bride's family blesses the couple as two families come together in love, trust and responsibility.
